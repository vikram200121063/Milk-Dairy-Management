Built for the day-to-day of running a dairy

OM Dairy is the operating system behind our milk collection and distribution business – from the first can collected at the village doorstep each morning, to the invoice a buyer settles weeks later. One place to track it, instead of three notebooks and a calculator.

What we collect

Twice-daily milk collection from every registered customer, weighed and graded on fat and SNF, priced automatically against our rate chart.

Who we supply

Bulk milk sold on to our buyers – hotels, sweet shops, and processing plants – billed on a half-month cycle with automatic reminders if a payment runs late.

What this app does

Keeps the books straight: what every customer is owed, what every buyer owes us, and the real cash position – all posted automatically as the day's entries come in.
